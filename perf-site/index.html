01 / ISOLATION

Mandatory strict sandbox

Linux Namespaces, Landlock filesystem rules, Seccomp syscall filtering, and default-deny egress stay enabled in every measured Capsid run.

02 / STARTUP

Sub-20 ms cold starts

Precompiled QuickJS bytecode eliminates parse overhead across 10 KiB to 1 MiB bundles while keeping process PSS in the low-tens of megabytes.

03 / EFFICIENCY

High throughput per MiB

No multi-hundred-MiB JIT code cache or runaway heap inflation: tight worker footprints translate directly into denser multi-tenant packing.

04 / PROVENANCE

Replay-verified evidence

Every verified point ships complete sealed logs, exact-body checks, recursive process-tree PSS proofs, and SHA-256 archive digests.

04 — Provenance

Methodology & evidence

Verified points require clean-tree Release measurements from bench/cold-start.sh, bench/qps-lane.sh, bench/density-lane.sh, and bench/collect-perf-point.py. Each point carries measured source and binary identities, a machine fingerprint, and revalidated archive digests. Historical observations without complete evidence are marked unverified. Evidence files for verified points are included in this site as complete hashed transports. Quiet WSL guest windows do not prove physical Windows-host exclusivity.
