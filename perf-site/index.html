04 — Provenance

Methodology & evidence

Points are produced from tagged, clean-tree Release builds by bench/cold-start.sh, bench/qps-lane.sh, and bench/collect-perf-point.py. Each point carries the machine fingerprint and SHA-256 digests of every binary and fixture used. Historical points are never edited; methodology changes start a new series rather than rewriting history.
